# 8.4. Internal Energy and the First Law

> [!abstract] In brief
> Internal energy.

## Key ideas

- Internal energy.
- Heat and work as modes of energy transfer.
- The first law of thermodynamics.
- The work done by a gas as an area on a pV diagram.
- The heat capacities of a gas.
