# 8. Molecular and Statistical Physics, Thermodynamics

> [!info] Section
> 8 cards · about 34 planned pages · level: in-depth

## Cards

- [[8.1 · The Molecular Picture and Temperature|8.1. The Molecular Picture and Temperature]] — The molecular picture connects observable properties of matter to motion and interaction of microscopic particles.
- [[8.2 · Kinetic Theory of an Ideal Gas|8.2. Kinetic Theory of an Ideal Gas]] — In the kinetic model, ideal-gas particles are small compared with their separations and interact only through elastic collisions.
- [[8.3 · Distributions, Fluctuations, and Diffusion|8.3. Distributions, Fluctuations, and Diffusion]] — Collisions continually redistribute energy among molecules, so equal temperature does not mean equal speed.
- [[8.4 · Internal Energy and the First Law|8.4. Internal Energy and the First Law]] — Internal energy U contains microscopic kinetic and potential contributions within the chosen system and is a state function.
- [[8.5 · Heating, Phase Transitions, and Heat Transfer|8.5. Heating, Phase Transitions, and Heat Transfer]] — Within one phase and over a modest interval, heat is often estimated as Q = mc Delta T, where c depends on substance and process conditions.
- [[8.6 · Thermodynamic Processes|8.6. Thermodynamic Processes]] — Isochoric, isobaric, isothermal, and adiabatic processes impose different constraints: fixed V, p, T, or no heat transfer, respectively.
- [[8.7 · Entropy and the Second Law|8.7. Entropy and the Second Law]] — A macrostate is fixed by a few measured variables, whereas a microstate specifies all microscopic degrees of freedom.
- [[8.8 · Heat Engines, Refrigerators, and Free Energy|8.8. Heat Engines, Refrigerators, and Free Energy]] — A heat engine receives Q_h from a hot reservoir, delivers work W, and rejects Q_c to a cold reservoir.
