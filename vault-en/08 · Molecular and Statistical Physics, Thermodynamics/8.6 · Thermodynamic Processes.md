# 8.6. Thermodynamic Processes

> [!abstract] In brief
> Isochoric, isobaric, isothermal, and adiabatic processes.

## Key ideas

- Isochoric, isobaric, isothermal, and adiabatic processes.
- Quasistatic change.
- A cycle on a pV diagram.
- Work, heat, and internal energy along each leg of a process.
- Real and idealized processes.

Define the system, name the measurable quantities, and check which assumptions allow these ideas to be applied. Connect the verbal description to a diagram, graph, and dimensional analysis.
