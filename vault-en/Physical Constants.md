# Physical Constants

| Symbol | Quantity | Value | Note |
|---|---|---:|---|
| $c$ | Speed of light in vacuum | 299792458 m/s | Exact SI defining value. |
| $G$ | Gravitational constant | 6.6743e-11 m³/(kg·s²) | CODATA 2022; the standard uncertainty is 0.00015e−11 m³/(kg·s²). |
| $h$ | Planck constant | 6.62607015e-34 J·s | Exact SI defining value. |
| $\hbar$ | Reduced Planck constant | 1.054571817e-34 J·s | Rounded decimal representation of the exact relation ħ = h/(2π). |
| $e$ | Elementary charge | 1.602176634e-19 C | Exact SI defining value; the electron's charge is −e. |
| $k_{\mathrm B}$ | Boltzmann constant | 1.380649e-23 J/K | Exact SI defining value. |
| $N_{\mathrm A}$ | Avogadro constant | 6.02214076e+23 mol⁻¹ | Exact SI defining value. |
| $R$ | Molar gas constant | 8.31446261815324 J/(mol·K) | The exact product N_A k_B. |
| $\varepsilon_0$ | Vacuum permittivity | 8.8541878188e-12 F/m | CODATA 2022; since the redefinition of the SI, it is a measured derived quantity. |
| $\mu_0$ | Vacuum permeability | 0.00000125663706127 N/A² | CODATA 2022; since the redefinition of the SI, it is no longer exactly equal to 4π·10⁻⁷ N/A². |
| $\alpha$ | Fine-structure constant | 0.0072973525643 dimensionless | CODATA 2022; it characterizes the strength of the electromagnetic interaction. |
| $m_e$ | Electron mass | 9.1093837139e-31 kg | CODATA 2022 recommended value. |
| $m_p$ | Proton mass | 1.67262192595e-27 kg | CODATA 2022 recommended value. |
| $m_n$ | Neutron mass | 1.67492750056e-27 kg | CODATA 2022 recommended value. |
| $m_u$ | Atomic mass constant | 1.66053906892e-27 kg | CODATA 2022; also denoted by u or the dalton (Da). |
| $g_0$ | Standard acceleration due to gravity | 9.80665 m/s² | Exact conventional value; the local acceleration due to gravity depends on location. |
| $\sigma$ | Stefan–Boltzmann constant | 5.670374419e-8 W/(m²·K⁴) | Rounded decimal representation of the value derived from the exact c, h, and k_B. |
| $\mathrm{eV}$ | Electronvolt in joules | 1.602176634e-19 J | Exact conversion: 1 eV is the energy acquired by a charge e when it passes through a potential difference of 1 V. |

Convert all quantities to a consistent system of units before calculating.
