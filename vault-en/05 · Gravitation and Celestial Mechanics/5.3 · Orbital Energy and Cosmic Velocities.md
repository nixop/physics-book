> [!abstract] In brief
> Circular and elliptical orbits.

## Key ideas

- Circular and elliptical orbits.
- The first and second cosmic velocities: circular-orbit speed and escape speed.
- Bound and unbound trajectories.
- Changing an orbit with a brief impulse.
