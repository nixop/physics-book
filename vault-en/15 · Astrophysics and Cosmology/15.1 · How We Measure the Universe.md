# 15.1. How We Measure the Universe

> [!abstract] In brief
> Angular sizes and parallax.

## Key ideas

- Angular sizes and parallax.
- Standard candles.
- Luminosity, flux, and magnitude.
- Spectrum, temperature, composition, and radial velocity.
- Redshift.
