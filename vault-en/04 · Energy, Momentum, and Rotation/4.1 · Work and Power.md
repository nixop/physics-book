# 4.1. Work and Power

> [!abstract] In brief
> Work done by a constant or variable force.

## Key ideas

- Work done by a constant or variable force.
- The role of the component of force along the displacement.
- Work as the area under a force graph.
- Power and the rate of energy transfer.
