## Cards

- [[1.1 · Physical Quantities, Units, and Scales|1.1. Physical Quantities, Units, and Scales]] — A physical quantity is defined through comparison with an accepted unit.
- [[1.2 · Measurement Error and Uncertainty|1.2. Measurement Error and Uncertainty]] — Measurement links an instrument indication to a quantity through calibration and a measurement model.
- [[1.3 · Data, Statistics, and Model Fitting|1.3. Data, Statistics, and Model Fitting]] — Raw data become a physical result after the procedure is documented, the distribution is visualized, and predictions are compared with observations.
- [[1.4 · Functions and Graphs|1.4. Functions and Graphs]] — A function assigns an outcome to an independent variable while other conditions are fixed.
- [[1.5 · Scalars and Vectors|1.5. Scalars and Vectors]] — A scalar is specified by a value with a unit and remains unchanged when coordinates are rotated, whereas a vector also carries a geometrical direction.
- [[1.6 · Projections, Components, and Vector Products|1.6. Projections, Components, and Vector Products]] — A projection asks how much of a vector points along a selected axis.
- [[1.7 · The Derivative, the Integral, and Equations of Change|1.7. The Derivative, the Integral, and Equations of Change]] — A derivative is the limiting ratio of a small change in a quantity to a small change in its argument.
