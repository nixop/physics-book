# 12. Spacetime and Relativity

> [!info] Section
> 6 cards · about 26 planned pages · level: advanced

## Cards

- [[12.1 · Why Classical Mechanics Proved Insufficient|12.1. Why Classical Mechanics Proved Insufficient]] — Newtonian kinematics treats time as common to all observers, so changing frames changes a velocity according to u' = u - v.
- [[12.2 · Simultaneity and Light Clocks|12.2. Simultaneity and Light Clocks]] — Distant clocks cannot be compared without a convention for transferring time.
- [[12.3 · Length Contraction and Lorentz Transformations|12.3. Length Contraction and Lorentz Transformations]] — Length is the distance between endpoint positions measured simultaneously in the frame where the answer is required.
- [[12.4 · Spacetime Diagrams and Causality|12.4. Spacetime Diagrams and Causality]] — A Minkowski diagram represents an event by coordinates x and ct and an object's history by a worldline.
- [[12.5 · Relativistic Energy and Momentum|12.5. Relativistic Energy and Momentum]] — For a particle of invariant mass m, relativistic momentum is p = γmv and total energy is E = γmc².
- [[12.6 · From the Equivalence Principle to Curved Spacetime|12.6. From the Equivalence Principle to Curved Spacetime]] — In a sufficiently small freely falling laboratory, the uniform part of a gravitational field can be removed by choosing the frame: unsupported bodies move inertially there.
