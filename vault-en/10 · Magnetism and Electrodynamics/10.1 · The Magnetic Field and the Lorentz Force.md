# 10.1. The Magnetic Field and the Lorentz Force

> [!abstract] In brief
> Magnetic poles and the absence of observed magnetic monopoles.

## Key ideas

- Magnetic poles and the absence of observed magnetic monopoles.
- The field of a moving charge.
- The Lorentz force.
- The direction of the force and the work done by a magnetic field.
