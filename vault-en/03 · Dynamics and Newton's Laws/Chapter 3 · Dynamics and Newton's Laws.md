# 3. Dynamics and Newton's Laws

> [!info] Section
> 7 cards · about 32 planned pages · level: foundation

## Cards

- [[3.1 · Interactions and Force Diagrams|3.1. Interactions and Force Diagrams]] — Force is not something stored inside a body; it is a vector description of a particular interaction.
- [[3.2 · Inertia and Newton's First Law|3.2. Inertia and Newton's First Law]] — The first law rejects the idea that force maintains velocity.
- [[3.3 · Newton's Second Law|3.3. Newton's Second Law]] — Newton's second law connects the cause of motion change to the momentum derivative: ΣFext = dp/dt for a material system of fixed composition.
- [[3.4 · Newton's Third Law|3.4. Newton's Third Law]] — If body A acts on B, B simultaneously acts on A with an equal and opposite force.
- [[3.5 · Normal Force, Tension, and Elasticity|3.5. Normal Force, Tension, and Elasticity]] — A support, string, or spring restricts possible motion and develops force through contact or deformation.
- [[3.6 · Friction and Drag|3.6. Friction and Drag]] — Friction and drag emerge from many microscopic interactions and are described by effective models.
- [[3.7 · Constraints, Circular Motion, and Non-Inertial Frames|3.7. Constraints, Circular Motion, and Non-Inertial Frames]] — A constraint limits allowed positions and velocities, while support or tension forces provide the required acceleration.
