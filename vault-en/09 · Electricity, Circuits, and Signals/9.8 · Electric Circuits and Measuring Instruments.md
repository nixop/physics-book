# 9.8. Electric Circuits and Measuring Instruments

> [!abstract] In brief
> Series and parallel connections.

## Key ideas

- Series and parallel connections.
- Kirchhoff's circuit laws.
- Ammeters, voltmeters, and the effect of a measuring instrument on a circuit.
- Short circuits and electrical safety.

## Working model

Define the system, name the measurable quantities, and check which assumptions allow these ideas to be applied. Connect the verbal description to a diagram, graph, and dimensional analysis.
