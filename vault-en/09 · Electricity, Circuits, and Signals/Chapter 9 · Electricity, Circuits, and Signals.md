# 9. Electricity, Circuits, and Signals

> [!info] Section
> 9 cards · about 34 planned pages · level: in-depth

## Cards

- [[9.1 · Electric Charge and Matter|9.1. Electric Charge and Matter]] — Electric charge sets the sign and strength of electromagnetic interaction.
- [[9.2 · Coulomb's Law and the Electric Field|9.2. Coulomb's Law and the Electric Field]] — Coulomb's law gives the vacuum force between stationary point charges: magnitude falls as $1/r^2$ and direction lies along their separation.
- [[9.3 · Electric Flux and Gauss's Law|9.3. Electric Flux and Gauss's Law]] — Electric flux through an oriented surface is the integral of $\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf A$ and counts only normal field.
- [[9.4 · Electric Potential and Voltage|9.4. Electric Potential and Voltage]] — For an electrostatic field, work between two points is path-independent, so potential energy U and potential $\varphi = U/q$ can be defined for a probe charge.
- [[9.5 · Conductors, Dielectrics, and Capacitors|9.5. Conductors, Dielectrics, and Capacitors]] — At electrostatic equilibrium, field inside the bulk of an ideal conductor is zero, its potential is constant, and excess free charge lies on its surface.
- [[9.6 · Current, Resistance, and the Microscopic Picture|9.6. Current, Resistance, and the Microscopic Picture]] — Current $I = dQ/dt$ measures charge transfer rate through a selected cross-section, while current density J describes it locally.
- [[9.7 · Sources, Power, and Energy Transfer|9.7. Sources, Power, and Energy Transfer]] — A source's electromotive force is work by non-electrostatic processes per unit charge moved inside the source.
- [[9.8 · Electric Circuits and Measuring Instruments|9.8. Electric Circuits and Measuring Instruments]] — In series, one current passes through every element and voltages add.
- [[9.9 · RC Circuits, Filters, and Signals|9.9. RC Circuits, Filters, and Signals]] — Ideal-capacitor voltage cannot jump because $Q=CV$ and finite current moves finite charge.
